# Research publication standard

Public research examples explain our work, not a customer's private life or endorsement. No additional case-publication checkbox is part of the intake flow. The existing required consent remains limited to research, relevant office contact and result delivery; the privacy notice is not a publication license.

Before publishing or expanding a research example:

1. Separate facts from lawfully accessible public records, our own research methods, and confidential customer-supplied context. Keep correspondence, contact information, nonpublic personal plans and feedback out of the public narrative unless a separate appropriate basis has been established.
2. Review combinations of county, exact dates, permit numbers, lot geometry and unusual circumstances for identifiability. Removing a name alone does not establish anonymization. Do not assume a customer controls an owner's or document creator's rights.
3. Keep supported findings and material uncertainty accurate. Do not invent success, a completed transaction, a satisfied customer, agency endorsement or the cause of a discrepancy.
4. Review images separately from factual summaries. Record the source, creator/rightsholder where known, applicable license or other proposed legal basis, excerpt purpose and identity review. Redaction is a privacy measure, not copyright clearance. Fair use is case-specific, not automatic because a page is educational or masked.
5. If image use is not cleared, prepare an original factual table/explanation or a lawful link. Do not trace distinctive source artwork and treat that as permission. Any decision to withdraw an existing asset must cover its static URL and deployed caches, not only the visible link.
6. Preserve earlier confidentiality promises and consent snapshots. A revised privacy policy does not retroactively authorize publication. A concern about an identifiable example should receive individual review; no unimplemented automatic deletion promise is made.

## Current release boundary

The 2026-09-17 form/privacy revision adds a plain-language research-use notice without an optional publication checkbox, and removes selected nonpublic project context/feedback from case narratives. The existing third-party source images have **not** received blanket copyright clearance and were not removed or re-licensed in this change. Their specific use remains subject to the separate source-rights review recorded privately. Do not describe this revision as full legal clearance, completed anonymization, or completed source-image replacement.

This standard is an operating checklist, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.
