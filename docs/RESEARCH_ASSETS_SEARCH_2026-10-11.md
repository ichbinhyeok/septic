# Research assets → search entrances

## Scope

Convert existing public findings and verified search procedures into useful answers on existing URLs. Add one distinct platform guide, not another generic county cluster. No new customer originals, identifiers, email captures or agency submissions are included.

- Drawing-reading guide: planned vs final record comparison (published Anderson findings: 300 ft planned, 200 ft final recorded), plus tank gallons vs written bedroom approval.
- As-built and permit-process guides: reuse the source-stage comparison; link to the reading guide.
- Bedroom checker: explain why the published tank/flow figures did not establish bedroom approval.
- Missing-record guide: link alternate local archive routes and the platform-specific empty-search guide.
- Davidson County TN: correct conflicting statewide-first steps/FAQ, document HealthCode parcel normalization, results limits, page identity and local fallback.
- St. Croix: distinguish SAN documents, maintenance history and Property Files; never imply a failed portal means no record.
- TDEC: expose the Davidson-specific archive without representing it as a statewide substitute.
- New `/septicsearch-records/`: county-first platform search, actual input fields, scanned-record interpretation, missing-file escalation, separately attributed missing/review intake links.
- Discovery links: Florida, official lookup tools, OnlineRME and missing-record guide. Existing titles are preserved; materially changed pages get specific sitemap revisions.

## Editorial guardrails and follow-up

One page owns each broad intent. Keep comparison questions on existing reading/approval pages rather than splitting near-duplicate URLs without measured demand. Platform guide is a content hypothesis, not a proven traffic winner. Official instructions, opened endpoint, submitted request and returned file are different evidence stages.

After deployment, log release date and compare complete 28-day GSC/Bing windows. Track impressions/queries for the new platform URL and question-led sections; inspect official-route opens separately from record-help submissions. Do not claim traffic gains before measurement. Any analytics review must create the required immutable operations growth snapshot.
