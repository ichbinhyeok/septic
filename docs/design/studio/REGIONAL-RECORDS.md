# Specialized regional records family

Preview routes:

- `/design-preview/studio/texas-ossf-records/`
- `/design-preview/studio/south-carolina-records/`
- `/design-preview/studio/florida-ostds-records/`

The family preserves each program's routing distinction while replacing the inherited regional portal presentation with one image-led records workspace. Texas begins with the authorized agent, South Carolina with a usable TMS or permit identifier, and Florida with the split between current permit authority and historical county archives.

Each preview uses the existing reviewed county route collections. County, identifier, secondary clue and approximate year stay in the browser; they are not submitted. A completed workspace opens the verified official source, the county instruction route and the modern full request builder. The request-builder handoff carries state, county, address and office context in browser storage.

Visual thesis: a rural-property hero establishes place, a cardless ruled workspace handles the task, and real-looking permit/as-built/final-approval imagery explains why the useful product is the complete file rather than one search result.

Production canonicals, sitemap entries and original routes remain unchanged until explicit cutover.
