# State replacement cost family

## Visual thesis

A field-report editorial with the calm finish of a premium property publication: landscape imagery creates atmosphere, while plans, files and ruled cost sections make the decision feel researched rather than advertised.

## Content plan

1. Full-bleed property hero with one scope-led promise and one estimate action.
2. Repair-versus-replacement framing that prevents a price from outrunning the diagnosis.
3. Site, system and restoration scope shown through an image-led editorial sequence.
4. SepticPath research proof and local workflow context.
5. Quote-preparation, official sources and a direct property-research CTA.

## Interaction thesis

- A restrained hero entrance inherited from the shared studio shell.
- Cost-driver rows sharpen on hover without becoming cards.
- Local rules, sources and related guides disclose progressively.

## Reuse policy

Strong property and research images may repeat across states. They are illustrative visual anchors, never claims that a pictured parcel belongs to the named state or county.

## Route family

All 50 published state replacement guides render at `/design-preview/studio/replacement-cost/{stateSlug}/`. The earlier outgoing-link audit exposed 33 legacy destinations, but the underlying launch-quality dataset contains all 50 states; keeping one complete visual family avoids a split experience.

## Verification

- All 50 state routes retain distinct state copy, cost scope, official sources and calculator handoff in the MVC suite.
- The discoverable studio crawl reaches 640 pages with zero HTTP, H1 or noindex failures; 32 replacement pages are reached through currently published editorial links, while all 50 direct routes are tested.
- The studio asset audit covers 72 local image, script and stylesheet URLs with zero missing responses.
- Georgia, Texas and Washington were rendered at 1440px and 390px. All six layouts have one visible H1, no page-width overflow, no broken images and no undersized inputs.
- Georgia verifies the state-cost-profile cohort with a planning-anchor section. Texas verifies the conservative cohort without invented state figures.
