# State drain-field replacement cost family

## Visual thesis

A premium field-report editorial that alternates broad land photography with plans and permit files to reveal the scope hidden below an ordinary yard.

## Content plan

1. Full-bleed parcel hero with a field-specific planning promise.
2. Separate visible symptoms from the diagnosis and official file.
3. Explain existing field, reserve area, excavation, distribution and restoration scope.
4. Show SepticPath's investigation method and the state/local decision route.
5. Prepare the quote conversation, preserve official sources, and close with property research.

## Interaction thesis

- Restrained hero entrance inherited from the studio shell.
- Scope rows shift slightly on hover to clarify sequence.
- Local workflow, source and related-route details disclose progressively.

## Cost boundary

The generic state installation and replacement midpoints are not presented as drain-field prices. The page hands the user's state and project type to the dedicated estimator instead of relabeling broader anchors.

## Route family

Fourteen state pages currently meet the public data-quality contract and render at `/design-preview/studio/drain-field-cost/{stateSlug}/`. The fifteenth legacy destination in the outgoing-link audit is the national guide, not another state route. States without a launch-quality drain-field page are not fabricated to complete a fifty-state set.
