# State perc-test cost family

## Visual thesis

A calm field-study editorial that combines premium land imagery with the precision of a soil-evaluation notebook. The ground is the protagonist; costs appear only after the site question is clear.

## Content plan

1. Full-bleed parcel hero with a single site-readiness promise.
2. A ruled explanation of what the test answers, what it cannot establish, and what follows.
3. Image-led soil, design and local-review scope.
4. State planning anchors only where the underlying cost profile exists.
5. SepticPath research proof, local workflow, official sources and final inquiry.

## Interaction thesis

- Restrained hero entrance from the shared studio shell.
- Decision rows shift subtly on hover to make the sequence legible.
- Local workflow and source detail disclose progressively.

## Reuse policy

Property and research imagery is illustrative and may repeat across states. It never claims to depict the named jurisdiction or a customer's parcel.
