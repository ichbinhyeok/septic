# Record Help: optional US $29 result unlock

## Customer value and language

Lead with the customer's question: missing permit or drawing, recorded capacity,
approval versus completed installation, repair history, conflicting claims, or
understanding a file they already have. Keep all customer groups and research
routes open. The promise is to find the official evidence, check that it belongs
to the property, and explain what it answers and what remains unknown.

Show existing public examples early, with their existing anonymization and
historical limits. Do not add unverified testimonials, success rates or promises
of record availability. Research and necessary agency requests remain free.
The preview states a real finding and its limits; US $29 buys the source package
and detailed answers. For uploaded files, sell the interpretation, not access
back to the customer's own documents. Payment instructions and delivery are by
email, with delivery after confirmed payment. No automatic download is promised.

## Accepted offer

This is the accepted launch policy and supersedes earlier suggestions to limit
customers, counties, questions, agencies or free request counts. Submission,
research, document review and necessary agency requests are free. A useful
verified result may be offered for US $29 after a free preview. No subscription,
upfront service fee, automatic charge, or complexity surcharge is authorized.
Agency search/copy/portal charges alone are additional, at actual cost and with
customer approval before incurring them. An approved agency fee may be payable
even when the agency finds nothing or the customer declines the unlock.

## Existing free customers

The existing 21 cases and all other intakes accepted under the previous free
offer retain free service for their original request, including late agency
returns, explanations and corrections. Do not rewrite historical intakes.
New intake files store an immutable offer snapshot with version
`record-help-optional-unlock-29-v1`, USD and 2900 cents. The absence of this
snapshot is NOT permission to charge. Check the original terms and linked case
before creating an offer, including duplicate intakes and returning customers.
Never use a customer's deadline or a delayed agency return to change free terms.

## Result decision

1. Research and request the necessary sources under OPERATIONS.md.
2. Match the exact property; review every page and preserve source hashes.
3. Write the question, the substantive answer supported by official evidence,
   and the remaining uncertainty. A result may be a permit, drawing, repair
   history, verified official status, conflict resolution, or useful explanation
   of a customer-supplied file. Never lock the customer's own access to their file.
4. Pending requests, receipts, generic metadata, portal no-match, blocked access,
   wrong-property files and insufficient identity evidence do not qualify.
   An official status or written no-record response can qualify only when it
   materially addresses the actual question. Disclose its conclusion, search
   scope and limits for free; do not imply physical absence or final approval.
5. Run the mandatory delivery gate for any preview or final message that relies
   on a customer-case file, even without an attachment. Require a fresh PASS for
   the exact recipient, subject and artifacts. Payment never bypasses this gate.
6. Preview the real finding, source/date, document extent, property match,
   answered questions, unresolved questions and important adverse findings.
   State exactly what the optional US $29 purchase contains.

## Manual payment and delivery

The application currently collects intakes; it does not process payments or
automatically unlock private documents. A configured merchant payment service
is required before sending an actionable payment offer. Never invent a checkout
URL, ask for card details by email, or treat a screenshot/return URL as payment.

Use a provider-hosted, one-time USD 29 checkout or invoice linked privately to
the case and offer version. Keep customer records off public checkout pages.
Verify completed payment in the merchant account (amount, currency, transaction
ID and matching offer) before delivery. Store the actual payment evidence and
delivery evidence using the operations transaction, not a second status ledger.
There is no automatic send: follow OPERATIONS.md authorization rules.

After verified payment, send the already reviewed original sources and answers,
using the delivery gate again if the receipt expired or any delivery detail
changed. Do not promise immediate automatic download in this manual workflow.
If delivery cannot be fulfilled or materially differs from the preview, correct
it or refund the service fee through the provider; retain the real outcome.

## Learning and review

Track intake offer version, route IDs, source outcome, operator minutes for all
work (including unpaid work), request/response dates, agency fees approved/paid,
preview date and scope, offer/transaction IDs, amount/currency, payment confirmed,
delivery confirmed, feedback and refunds. Keep identifiable data in private
storage. Do not send names, addresses, parcel IDs or source files to analytics.

minutes / paid orders, and why customers paid or declined. Pending work is not
a failed search; a positive reply is not a payment. No price or scope changes
are automatic. Keep the US $29 terms already shown to each customer.
